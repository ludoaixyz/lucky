## Game configuration

Document reels, rows, pay model, configuration profile, enabled features, and versioned source files.

## Bet model

Document integer line bet, active lines/ways, total bet, denomination assumptions, and rounding rules.

## Symbol inventory

List stable ID, name, category, substitution/scatter behavior, and display role.

## Reel-strip summary and reel weighting

For each reel record stop count, per-symbol counts, weighting method, and independent-selection assumptions.

## Paytable

List symbol, match count, integer award, direction, wild rules, and exceptions.

## Paylines or ways

Define every row path or ways rule, bet convention, and evaluation order.

## Base-game hit frequency

State method, inputs, formula, result, and whether exact or simulated.

## Feature trigger frequency

Describe trigger combinations, probability, re-trigger treatment, and contribution method.

## Free-spin contribution

Document awarded spins, multipliers, alternate reels/paytable, re-triggers, and expected contribution.

## RTP decomposition

Break out base line wins, scatters, bonuses, free spins, jackpots (if any), and caps.

## Total theoretical RTP

Record exact methodology, result, precision, and independent verification. Do not populate until verified.

## Volatility methodology

Define return units, moment calculations, sample/enumeration scope, variance, standard deviation, and any index.

## Payout distribution

Provide mutually exclusive payout-multiple buckets, counts/probabilities, and reconciliation total.

## Maximum exposure and maximum win

Describe uncapped maximum exposure, configured cap, maximum credited win, reachability proof, and feature interaction.

## Simulation settings and results

Record build fingerprint, engine version, spins, seed(s), batch method, wager/payout totals, hits, triggers, and runtime. Keep results separate from theoretical claims.

## Confidence intervals

State confidence level, estimator, assumptions, standard error, bounds, and adequacy judgment.

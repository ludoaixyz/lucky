# Math model

For paid outcome `i` with probability `p_i`, five-credit bet `b`, base payout `B_i`, and all resulting free-spin payout `F_i`, feature-inclusive return is `X_i = (B_i + F_i) / b`. Base line RTP is `E[B_line]/b`; base Scatter RTP is zero while direct Scatter credits are disabled; feature RTP is `E[F]/b`; total RTP is their sum. Free spins never create another wager or paid trial.

Five fixed paylines evaluate left-to-right. Wild may substitute for regular line symbols. `SCATTER` is counted anywhere in the full 5x3 window and neither Wild nor Scatter substitutes for the other or for regular line symbols. Three, four, and five Scatters award 8, 10, and 12 initial free spins. During free spins they add 5, 8, and 10 spins. The feature uses a 1x multiplier, shared strips/paytable, at most 100 played spins and at most 20 successful retriggers.

`npm run math:enumerate` visits all 248,832 paid-stop combinations. Method A reuses that exact outcome distribution for free spins and memoizes the finite state `(remaining spins, played spins, retrigger count)`. It therefore terminates deterministically. Exact trigger probability, expected feature value and length, uncapped reward moments, and low payout-bucket CDFs follow from those state equations. The aggregate maximum-win cap is enforced once by runtime spin resolution after base and feature aggregation. The PAR labels exact return moments as uncapped and uses capped Monte Carlo for the credited comparison; it does not claim an exact cap-tail adjustment.

Monte Carlo treats one iteration as one paid spin plus its entire feature. The return-multiple population variance is `E[(X-E[X])²]`, standard deviation is in bet multiples, standard error is `s/sqrt(n)`, and the displayed 95% RTP interval is `mean ± 1.96*SE`, lower-clamped to zero. It is repeatable but approximate. Seeded Mulberry32 supports tests and offline study, not production or regulatory randomness. Figures are engineering results, not certification.
